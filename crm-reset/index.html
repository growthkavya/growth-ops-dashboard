The CRM Reset

We went through our entire LeadSquared system end to end: every one of our 167,584 leads, all 234 data fields, all 22 automations, and 209 complete lead histories, activity by activity. This page is what we found and what we are going to do about it. Ten minutes to read, no technical language. Nothing here is opinion; every number comes from that read.

167,584leads in the system today
234data fields per lead. About 80 are dead weight nobody fills.
22automations running, many fighting each other
40%of the entire database is marked "Not Interested"
50%of all leads sit under a single login

What is going wrong today

Seven things, each explained the way we would explain it across a table.

1. We cannot say where a lead came from

When leadership asks "which ad or video brought this student?", the honest answer is: we usually do not know. Twelve different fields each hold a piece of the answer, none is complete, and when we retarget someone, the new campaign overwrites the record of what originally brought them in. So every spend debate runs on gut feel.

1 in 20leads has a protected "first source"

2. People who said "call me later" get buried with wrong numbers

When a student says "I will buy after my exam" or "fees are tight this month", the system files them in the same bucket as spam and wrong numbers: Not Interested. Nothing ever resurfaces them, so nobody calls back at the right time. A large part of these 66,598 people are future customers with a stated reason to return.

66,598leads in the Not Interested bucket

3. Paid students turn back into brand-new leads

One faulty automation wipes a lead's status whenever they fill any form. A student who already paid and just downloaded a brochure becomes a "new lead" again and can land in a calling queue. Even people who asked us never to call them get recycled. This is why statuses feel unreliable: the system itself is erasing them.

2 of 3status changes are made by automations, not people

4. Half the database is parked under one login

Every lead that cannot be assigned defaults to one account, which now holds 84,243 leads. Hot leads age there uncalled, and nobody can fairly measure counsellor workload while half the base sits in one place. The team already flagged this in the intake sheet; the read confirmed it is built into two automations.

84,243leads under one login

5. The CRM does not know about a third of our sales

It shows 8,148 closed sales. We actually made roughly 13,000. Payments are recorded by hand, when someone remembers, so the system cannot run the follow-ups that matter most: congratulating a student who passed CFA Level 1 and offering Level 2 at the right moment.

~5,000sales the CRM never saw

6. One student can only have one status, even with two interests

A student who is hot for CFA Level 1 and lukewarm about FRM has exactly one status field. Whichever course was discussed last wins, and the other interest silently disappears. Multi-course students, our best customers, are invisible as multi-course students.

1status field per student today

7. Our email channel is in real trouble

Six in ten leads we sampled have had an email bounce, and unsubscribe rates are many times beyond healthy. Years of sending everything to everyone burned our sending reputation. Until this is repaired, even well-written emails quietly fail to arrive. There is an open support case with LeadSquared on this right now.

6 in 10sampled leads have a bounced email
And the smaller stuff, briefly:
  • Every call's recorded time is 5.5 hours off, because of a timezone mistake in an automation.
  • Our real segmentation lives in a field literally named "dummy", filled by hand on 86,000 leads.
  • One dropdown has 912 options, including "testing" and "28".
  • "CA Final Cohort" and "CA FINAL cohort" exist as two different groups (51,844 students split between them).
  • Five separate automations fire on every single logged call, in no guaranteed order.

What is NOT the problem: the people

Of 209 lead histories we checked, 196 had proper call logs. Counsellors are logging calls, 5 to 6 per lead. Duplicates are impressively low. And data captured in 2026 is far better than 2025. This is a system that grew one patch at a time for years. Nobody broke it on purpose, and nobody needs to be blamed for it. It needs a reset, not a culprit.

What the new LeadSquared will do

Here is the journey of one student through the rebuilt system. Each step shows what happens, and what happens today instead.

1

The moment a form is filled, we remember them properly

The system checks if we already know this person (no duplicate entries), and permanently records where they truly came from. That first answer can never be overwritten by later campaigns. What re-engaged them later is recorded separately.

Today: retargeting overwrites the original source, and the truth is gone.
2

Leads are sorted by heat, instantly

"Call me about CFA" is not the same as "send me the brochure". The system reads which form was filled, which course, and how close the exam is, and sorts each lead: call now, call today, call this week, or nurture automatically. Counsellor queues start with the hottest.

Today: every website lead lands in one New Lead pile with equal priority.
3

An instant, honest hello

Within a minute: a WhatsApp for their course with a real promise ("our counsellor will call you before 11 am tomorrow") and an email with the brochure and batch calendar. Hot leads during working hours get "we are calling you in 15 minutes", and we actually do.

Today: the welcome depends on which campaign the lead came from, if any.
4

The right counsellor, with a running clock

Leads route to counsellors who actually have capacity, with a deadline attached. Unassignable leads go to a named queue the manager clears daily. Nothing defaults to a person's inbox to age quietly.

Today: unassigned leads park under one login. 84,243 so far.
5

After every call: one 15-second form

Six quick fields: what happened, which course, exam window, any objection, next action, next date. That form, not anyone's memory, is what moves the lead forward. Typing essays into a comments box is over.

Today: a 23-field form everyone skips, and the substance goes into one long free-text note.
6

Every answer triggers the right next step, automatically

Said yes to a follow-up? The reminder is set and the student gets a WhatsApp the day before. Did not pick up? A polite retry ladder, then automated nurturing instead of endless redials. Said "after my exam"? They get a wake-up date and we call them then. Nobody worthwhile falls into a black hole.

Today: "call me later" means goodbye forever.
7

Each course gets its own track

A student interested in CFA Level 1 and FRM has two separate tracks, each with its own status, so being hot for one and cold for the other is finally possible. Messages go per course, so a CFA student never gets a random CA blast.

Today: one status field; the last conversation wins.
8

Payments close the loop by themselves

When a student pays, the payment gateway tells the CRM directly: course, amount, date. The sale closes automatically, onboarding messages go out, and revenue appears against the right campaign and counsellor without anyone typing it.

Today: sales are marked by hand, and about 5,000 never got marked at all.
9

Exam results open the next chapter

Passed Level 1? A congratulations message and a Level 2 conversation at exactly the right moment. Did not pass? A support call, not silence. Registered but did not appear? We know, and we follow up. This is where repeat business stops depending on memory.

Today: exam outcomes are barely recorded, so none of this can run.
10

Leadership wakes up to real numbers

Every morning at 8 am, automatically: yesterday's leads by source and course, whether we called people on time, sales matched against actual payments, and what moved in the pipeline. Weekly: which channels make money, why we lose leads, and whether the system is staying clean.

Today: reports are compiled by hand, from data nobody fully trusts.

What changes for each team

Counsellors

Shorter form (15 seconds, six fields), queues sorted hottest-first with promised call times, follow-ups scheduled for you, and no more essay-writing into notes. Your good work finally shows up in numbers.

Performance marketing

A real answer to "which campaign made money". First source is protected forever, last touch tracked separately, and a weekly spend-to-revenue view per channel. Meta form types become distinguishable.

Organic

Your posts, videos and webinars finally get credit. Post-level tracking through uniform link tags, webinar registrations visible as fresh activity even for old leads, and a monthly "what happened to organic leads" report.

Sales managers

A live scoreboard: speed to first call, follow-up discipline, conversions and revenue per counsellor. Plus no more leads rotting unassigned; anything stuck surfaces to you daily.

Leadership

One 8 am email a day with numbers you can trust, because they come from payments and system events, not manual updates. And one monthly view: this month's leads, and what became of them.

RevOps and tech

Half the fields, one data dictionary any vendor can read, clean cohort lists that maintain themselves, and a weekly automated health check so the mess never grows back.

One more thing: the requirements intake sheet the team filled recently was checked line by line against this plan. All 25 asks are covered. Nothing anyone asked for was dropped.

The plan, in five steps

Decide together

One working session locks the decisions: the new status list, the course list, the source list, and the rules. Everything after this is execution.

Build alongside, quietly

The new fields and structures are created next to the old ones. Nothing existing is touched. The CRM keeps running exactly as today.

Repoint the entrances

Every form, ad connector and import starts writing into the new clean structure, with fixed dropdown choices so typos and duplicates cannot creep back in.

Replace the automations, one at a time

The 22 overlapping automations become 9 purpose-built ones. Each old one is switched off only after its replacement is proven working. The stage-wiping bug dies in this step.

Clean the history, and keep it clean

Old data is migrated where trustworthy, duplicates merged, dead fields retired. Then a weekly automated health check and a one-page team SOP make sure it stays this way.

Nothing breaks during this. No data is deleted until its replacement is verified, the CRM stays live throughout, and every change is reversible until the final cleanup step. The riskiest thing we can do is nothing: the current system is actively erasing statuses and burying future customers every day.

What we need from you

Leadership: one hour for the decision session, and visible backing for the new call-form discipline. If the form is optional, the data dies again.

Counsellors: use the new 15-second form after every call, every time. It is the entire system's heartbeat, and it is shorter than what exists today.

Marketing teams (performance and organic): use the standard link tags on everything you publish. One naming sheet, provided.

Everyone: no new fields, lists or automations without a quick word with Growth Ops. That habit is how the 234-field situation happened.
