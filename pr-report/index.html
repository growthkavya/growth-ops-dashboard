1 · The short version

A note on numbers: the agency's report puts a ₹70.5 lakh "PR value" on this coverage. We don't use that figure. It prices each article as if we'd bought ad space, and counts a newspaper's entire readership as ours.

5 · What we need

Five decisions for leadership

The next 90 days mostly reuse people and tools we already have. What they need is these five decisions.

  1. Judge PR on trust, not article countsThe monthly three-step report replaces the agency's rupee "PR value".
  2. Time from Sanjay Sir and VidyutTwo to three hours a week between them for interviews, opinion pieces and podcasts.
  3. Make Talent Pulse a yearly study we own1,000+ of our own students, published on our website first.
  4. A new brief for the agencyFewer copies of one story; more national outlets, TV, podcasts, print and Bengali press.
  5. One person inside SSEI owns PRHolds the reporter list, the crisis plan and the monthly numbers.
