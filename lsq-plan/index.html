01 The mess we found
Problem

Our lead system grew one patch at a time

Leads now reach us through at least 15 doors: Meta and Google ads, the website, webinars, events, WhatsApp, colleges, partners, free downloads, counsellor calls and imported lists. Each door was added quickly, and the system was never redesigned around them. We read all of it, every lead and every setting, without changing anything. Issue 1: Leads are put in the wrong buckets

Proposed
Not Interestedreal person, said no

Marketing Junkunusable record only
What is happening

We have 12 lead stages and each team reads them differently. 40% of all leads are marked Not Interested, and 21% sit in a stage called "Marketing use", which is really a storage shelf. When a counsellor picks certain reasons, such as "Not looking for any course", the system automatically moves that person into Marketing Junk.

Why it matters

Marketing Junk is meant for bad data, not for real people, yet 4,505 genuine contacts were sent there. People who said "call me after my exam" are mixed with people who said a firm no, so nobody calls them back at the right time. A paid student who downloads a brochure is even reset to "New Lead".

What we will do

One short list of stages, each with a single clear meaning. Marketing Junk is used only for unusable records such as fake numbers or spam. Real people who say no, ask for the wrong course, or are not ready yet each get their own bucket, and "not ready yet" always comes with a date to call back. Full lists: Decisions 1 and 2.

How they arrived decides the source. What they did afterwards is a touchpoint.
02 Key issues and solutions · 3 of 5

Issue 3: Messy fields, uncontrolled imports, no rules on access

Which course a student wants is stored in 10 places
Course CategoryCourse NameCourse InterestCFA CoursesCA CoursesFRM CoursesProgram+3 moreOne field: Course Interested
How an imported list should be named
"Imported Lead"
"Event · Finance Career Utsav · Jan 2026"
What is happening

Every lead carries 234 fields and about 80 are almost never filled. The same information lives in several places, and one dropdown has 912 options, including "testing". Lists are imported by different people in different formats, often labelled only "Imported Lead". Nothing written says who may import, add fields or change the automatic rules.

Why it matters

Reports disagree with each other, duplicates creep in, and leads land with the wrong source or the wrong owner. Every new team member or vendor has to guess what each field means. Several automatic rules exist as "copy of a copy" because changes were made directly on the live system with no record.

What we will do

A field dictionary: every field gets one meaning, one owner and a fixed list of values. Every import goes through a checklist (format, duplicates, source, event name, approval, quality check). A small number of admins, with approval needed before any structural change. Full lists: Decisions 4 and 8.

What is happening

44% of first calls are logged under one label, "DNP / RNR" (did not pick up), with no reason. Recordings are sometimes not saved, not linked to the student, or completely silent. Call times are stored 5.5 hours off, because one automatic rule uses the wrong time zone.

Why it matters

Managers cannot tell whether a problem is the counsellor, the process, the phone system or simply the student's availability. Without recordings there is no quality check, no real material to train new counsellors, no way to settle "that is not what I was told", and no record of what students actually ask.

What we will do

Every failed call gets a reason, split into "our side" and "student's side". We investigate the recording problem with the telephony provider; the exact cause is to be validated. The time-zone setting gets fixed so call times and follow-up reports can be trusted.

02 Key issues and solutions · 5 of 5

Issue 5: We lose our best future customers

"Call me after my exam"
Never called back
Interested in CFA and FRM
Only the last course is kept
An alum fills a form
Reset to "New Lead"
What is happening

About 1,300 people said "later" or "fees are tight" and have no date to be called back. A student interested in two courses has only one stage, so the last course discussed overwrites the other. When any of our 36,895 alumni fills a form, an automatic rule resets them to "New Lead".

Why it matters

These are the people most likely to buy: students who are not ready yet, students interested in more than one course, and alumni ready for the next level. On our records, only 13% of CFA Level 1 students go on to buy Level 2 from us, and 10,557 students have already bought more than one course.

What we will do

Every valid lead gets a nurturing path and a return date instead of a dead end. Each course a student is interested in is tracked separately, with its own source and stage. The alumni label is protected so no call or form can remove it. Full lists: Decisions 6 and 7, and the cohortisation plan.

Decision 5

Decision 5: How long does the first channel keep the credit?

The first source never changes. This decision is about credit: if a student comes back through a different channel and then buys, which channel gets credit for the sale? A credit window sets the cut-off. The working assumption is 60 days, and it has not been confirmed.

Inside the window: Meta keeps the credit
Outside it: a new cycle, the webinar gets credit

No rule exists today

No window is written in any automatic rule or document we reviewed.

The team asked for 30 days

Performance marketing's requirement: keep the source fixed for 30 days, then let a new source take over.

What our data shows

In the Scholarship campaign, new students' share of sales fell from 78% in month one to 40% by month four. Buying ran for about 120 days.

Two things to decide: the length (30, 60, or 90 days and more), and what it applies to: credit for the sale only, or also when a returning lead is counted as "reactivated".

Rahul first reached us through a Meta ad for CFA Level 1. Two weeks later he registered for an FRM webinar. Today, the FRM interest would overwrite CFA. Under this proposal, his one profile holds a separate course track for each course, and each track keeps its own locked source and its own stage.

How a course gets tagged

From the course chosen on a form, the course an ad or webinar was about, or the course a counsellor records on the call form. A new course adds a new track; it never replaces an existing one.

How sources stay locked

The profile's first source is set on day one and never changes. Each course track also locks its own source the moment that course is first tagged, so we can see which channel created interest in which course.

How stages work

Each track moves through the stages on its own. Buying CFA converts only the CFA track; FRM carries on. The profile shows the most advanced track, and reports can count by person or by course.

Options: every course gets its own track, start with CFA and FRM only, or not now. Supporting facts: 10,557 students have already bought more than one course, and LeadSquared already has this feature ("opportunities") switched on but never set up.
