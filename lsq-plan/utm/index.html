SSEI UTM Dictionary

Every link we share that brings someone to SSEI carries five UTM fields. This page says what each field means, what goes in it, and the only values allowed, so that LeadSquared can record where every student really came from. It applies to every new link from today; old links are not edited.

The five fields, one job each

Every fact goes in exactly one field. The platform is never repeated in the campaign; the course is never repeated in the content; the month is never repeated anywhere else.

Format. Lowercase only. Hyphens inside a part, underscores between parts. No spaces, capitals, ampersands, slashes or names of students.
All five, every time. A link with only a source is a broken link. The builder below fills all five.
Only on links that bring people to us. Never on links between pages of our own site, and never on a direct visit.

Performance team: let the platforms fill the fields

Nobody types UTMs into individual ads. One line per platform, set once.

Meta

Meta ads that open our website

Paste into the ad's URL parameters box. Meta replaces the three macros on every click.

utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}
Meta

Meta instant forms

There is no click to our site, so UTMs do not apply. Leave tracking parameters and hidden fields empty. Campaign, ad set, ad and form names reach LeadSquared through the Meta connection. A macro typed into a form arrives as plain text, which is the likely cause of today's 22,723 broken values.

Google

Google Ads

Auto-tagging on. One final URL suffix at account level, and three custom parameters set on each campaign: {_source} google or youtube, {_medium} cpc, display or paid_video, {_campaign} the campaign name. Google has no macro for a campaign's name, which is why it is a custom parameter.

utm_source={_source}&utm_medium={_medium}&utm_campaign={_campaign}&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}
LinkedIn and others

LinkedIn and any other paid platform

Build the link with the builder above and paste it as the destination. If the platform offers dynamic UTM parameters, use them with the same five fields.

What LeadSquared does with your UTMs

A paid medium always wins. cpc, display, paid_video or paid_social gives Paid, even when the ad lands on a webinar or event page.
Otherwise an event or partner form decides. A webinar, event, scholarship or seminar registration gives Events or B2B / Partners, whatever unpaid medium brought them.
Otherwise the medium decides. As in the medium table.
No UTMs means Website, or Organic · Search engine or AI search when the visit came from Google, Bing, ChatGPT or similar.
First source is locked forever. The UTMs on a person's first visit set their first source, channel and campaign. Later visits update only the latest fields.
Credit for a sale. The first channel gets the credit if the sale comes within 60 days of the first visit. After that, the channel that brought them back does.
