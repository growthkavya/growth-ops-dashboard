The student meetup

Students stayed for more than four hours

The meetup was the centre of the trip, and the clearest sign that Mumbai wants more of SSEI.

More than 65 students came, along with 10 of our alumni, and the conversations ran for over four hours. Nobody was in a hurry to leave, and that tells us more than any feedback form could.

We promoted the evening ahead of time and got close to 200 sign-ups, with 150 people confirming they would come. Every one of those leads is now with the sales team, ready for follow-up calls, ad retargeting and invites to our next Mumbai event.

Two of our videos came out of this evening: students in conversation with Sir, and alumni on what learning with him was like.

What we learned

Five things we'll do differently next time

What worked, what didn't, and how we plan the next city trip because of it.

Expect about a third of sign-ups to come

That's normal, and now we have a number to plan the venue, food and follow-up around.

Mumbai meetup: ~200 signed up, 65+ came.

Plan the edit before the shoot

Editing now decides how fast our content goes out. Editors and timelines should be lined up before we press record.

28 videos and 4 podcasts shot in six days.

Always travel with a local

Someone who knows the city makes the trip personal. We reach the right places, see how people really live and shoot better content.

Keep a spare day

Only once we are in a place do we see what else is worth doing there. A packed itinerary leaves no room for those ideas.

Set targets we can actually hit

We aimed too high this time. A realistic daily target gets more finished work in the end.

Plan was 10 videos in one day. Not physically possible.
