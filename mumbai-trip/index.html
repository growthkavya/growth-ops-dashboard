The student meetup

Students stayed for more than four hours

The meetup was the centre of the trip. More than 65 students came, along with 10 of our alumni, and the conversations ran for over four hours. Nobody was in a hurry to leave, and that tells us more than any feedback form could.

We promoted it ahead of time and got close to 200 sign-ups, with 150 people confirming they would come. Every one of those leads is now with the sales team. We can follow up with them directly, retarget them in ads and invite them to our next Mumbai event.

Two of the videos below came out of this evening: students talking with Sir, and alumni talking about what learning with him was like.

What we learned

Five things we'll do differently next time

Expect about a third of sign-ups to show up

At the Mumbai meetup, close to 200 people signed up and 65+ came. That's about one in three, which is fine. It gives us a reliable number to plan the venue, food and follow-up around.

Plan the edit before the shoot

We shot 28 videos and 4 podcasts in six days, and editing is now what decides how fast they go out. Next time, editors and timelines get lined up before we start shooting.

Always travel with a local

Someone who knows the city makes the trip more personal. We get to the right places, see more of how people really live and shoot better content because of it.

Keep a spare day

Only once we are in a place do we see what else is worth doing there. A packed itinerary leaves no room for those ideas, so one free day should be built in.

Set targets we can actually hit

We aimed too high this time. Shooting 10 videos in a single day was not physically possible. A realistic daily target gets more finished work in the end.
